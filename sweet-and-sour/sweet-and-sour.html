Sweet & Sour

Sweet and sour pork the way my husband's grandmother made it. She was Chinese and the secret is apple cider vinegar. This recipe is the way Gramma made sweet and sour dishes her whole life. It is the best!

Ingredients:

Marinade

  • 1 Teaspoon Soy Sauce
  • 1 Teapoon Salt
  • 1/4 Teaspoon White Sugar
  • 2 Chopped Green Onions

Stir Fry

  • 1 tablespoon Vegetable OIl
  • Diced pork
  • 3 Stalks of Celery
  • 1 Green Bell Pepper
  • 1 Onion
  • 1 1/4 cups of water
  • 1/3 cup of white sugar
  • 1/3 cup of white vinegar
  • 1/4 cup of ketchup
  • 1/ teaspoon of soy sauce
  • 1 can of pineaaple chunks
  • 2 tablespoons of cornstarch

Step By Step:

  1. Dice/ prep ingredients
  2. Mix marinade
  3. Mix 1 cup water, 3/4 cup sugar, apple cider vinegar, ketchup, soy sauce, and salt in a large bowl
  4. Add pork to marianade and season to taste, leave for 30 minutes before coating in corn starch
  5. Add oil to medium/high heat and fry diced pork for aproximately 10 minutes, remove and set to the side for now
  6. Add more oil if necessary before frying up your vegetables until tender, aproximately 5 minutes, (add pineaple last)
  7. Add your sauce and bring to a boil for 2-3 minutes
  8. Re-add your pork for 20-30 seconds to quickly coat and reheat
  9. Plate up
